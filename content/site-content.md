## Intro

I am a software engineer at [WeRide](https://www.weride.ai/). I completed my PhD in
Industrial Engineering at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://www.wisc.edu/).
I was a member of [SIDA](https://kaibo.ie.wisc.edu/people.html) and have the guidance of
Prof. [Kaibo Liu](https://kaibo.ie.wisc.edu/). Before that, I received my B.Eng. in
Automation at [Xi'an Jiaotong University](http://en.xjtu.edu.cn/), where I was advised by
Prof. [Chao-Bo Yan](https://gr.xjtu.edu.cn/en/web/chaoboyan/english).

For more information about myself and my work, see my [curriculum vitae](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1mCUlqCV-zrhgpvoX90Mlu-SlskgE_yKt/view?usp=sharing).
